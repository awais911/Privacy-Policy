Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NZ Explorer (developed by NovaZen) handles your information. Your privacy is our top priority: NZ Explorer is a fully offline, standalone utility that does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal or device data.

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

NZ Explorer operates entirely on your local device. We do not require account registration, logins, or cloud synchronization. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • We do not track app usage, file access logs, or analytics.

2. App Permissions and Local Processing

To function as a complete file manager, NZ Explorer requires specific device permissions. All operations triggered by these permissions are processed exclusively on your device and are never sent over the internet:

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to browse, create, move, rename, delete, compress (ZIP), extract, and manage files across internal and external storage as directed by the user.
  • Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Used strictly to allow users to view installed applications and generate local backup copies (APKs). No package or application list data is ever recorded or shared externally.
  • Foreground Services: Used to maintain uninterrupted local operations such as batch file transfers and background audio/media playback when navigating away from the active screen.

3. No Advertising and No Paywalls

NZ Explorer contains:

  • No Advertisements: We do not integrate any third-party ad networks, advertising SDKs, or tracking beacons.
  • No Paywalls or In-App Purchases: All features are completely free and unlocked for all users.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Because NZ Explorer does not collect any data and has no external server infrastructure, your personal information and files are never sold, rented, traded, or shared with any third party.

5. Security of Your Files

Your files, documents, and media remain strictly within your device's file system or target directories you select. NZ Explorer does not upload or create cloud copies of any files.

6. Children’s Privacy

NZ Explorer does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for users of all ages due to its zero-data-collection design.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect any technical or policy updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
